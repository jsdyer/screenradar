Sources and privacy

TV searches and show details go directly to TVmaze. TV metadata is attributed to TVmaze under CC BY-SA and displays the source link on each result. Loading TV results can also load TVmaze’s cover images.

The film shelf uses selected Canadian records from Wikidata under CC0. It is a handpicked collection. Film filtering and the watchlist run on this device.

Follow the linked source for updated credits and release details. Schedules and viewing availability vary by region.
